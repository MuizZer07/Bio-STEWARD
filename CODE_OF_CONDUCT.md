# Code of conduct

Bio-STEWARD adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and keep discussions on what the
toolkit checks and how. Harassment, personal attacks and the publication of others' private information are not
accepted.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, within 7 days, by the
maintainer. The enforcement guidelines of the Covenant apply.
